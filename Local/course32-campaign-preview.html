Course 32 campaign: a sustained invitation to study and practice

A local visual board for Instagram, Facebook and Meta. The voice is aimed at mature Buddhist practitioners: spacious, specific and grounded in what the course offers, while making clear that prior Dzogchen study is not required.

Visual direction drawn from the existing Course 32 promotion: teal patterned field, gold frame, white panel and classic serif headings.

Audience

Mature practitioners who may value a sustained encounter with a primary Dzogchen text rather than isolated teachings. Prior Dzogchen study or familiarity with the Dra Thal Gyur is not required; registration includes the introductory class.

Traditional context

The Dra Thal Gyur is the root tantra of the Seventeen Tantras of the Dzogchen Upadeśa tradition. The planned journey unfolds through six twelve-week courses, beginning with Course 32.

Campaign execution begins today, September 9. Publish the Instagram post, Story carousel and Facebook post today. The Meta creative is ready for activation alongside them. This board is a local production plan only and does not publish or send anything.
