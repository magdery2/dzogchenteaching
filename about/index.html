About Lama Michael Gregory

Thirty years across the Indo-Tibetan and Theravāda traditions, guiding students from their first class toward the complete path to liberation.

A Path Begun in the Early 1990s

Lama Michael Gregory is a Buddhist teacher whose training spans more than three decades across the Indo-Tibetan and Theravāda traditions. Beginning his studies in the early 1990s, he apprenticed under many of the greatest meditation masters of Tibet, including HH The Dalai Lama, and his root teacher from his teenage years, HH Penor Rinpoche, at Namdroling Monastery.

His training was never purely academic. Years of sustained retreat, direct transmission, and daily practice sit underneath everything he teaches, from the first mindfulness class a student takes to the most advanced Dzogchen tantras. He holds the Longchenpa and Seminal Heart lineage of the Great Perfection, alongside deep grounding in Theravāda mindfulness and the Mahayana path of the bodhisattva.

Teaching the Complete Path

Lama Michael teaches the complete Buddhist path to liberation, following the traditional stages of Lam Rim: the graduated stages of the path to enlightenment. Rather than handing every student the same material, he personally guides each person from their first class toward the practices suited to them, whether that means beginning with a foundational mindfulness course or, for long-time practitioners, moving into the Dzogchen Great Perfection transmissions he teaches live most Tuesdays.

He founded Vajragarbha to make that same progression available to students anywhere, blending classical, precisely transmitted teachings with the accessibility of live and recorded online classes. He is joined in that work by a small circle of teachers he trained and trusts, including Lama Philip, Lama Kinley, and others across the Theravāda, Mahayana, Vajrayana, and Dzogchen traditions.
