For students

Who is this for?

For students ready to move from occasional inspiration into a lived Buddhist practice: to sit, study, question, and return again. Membership offers a rhythm in which steadiness, compassion, and discernment can be cultivated over time.

Questions

Before You Join

Do I need prior Dzogchen experience?

The membership contains teachings across the full path. If you are new, begin with the free mindfulness foundation and ask the teaching team which stage is right for you.

What if I cannot attend live?

Recordings and supporting materials are available through Teachable, allowing members in different time zones to study on their own schedule.

Where do I access the courses?

Member courses and recordings are hosted in the Vajragarbha teaching space on Teachable. Current members can use the Student Login link above.

Is financial assistance available?

Yes. Students can request support through the financial support form. Donations help make membership, courses, readings, and retreats more accessible.
