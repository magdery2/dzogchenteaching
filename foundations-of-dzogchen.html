The Foundations of Dzogchen

A Clear Foundation for the Dzogchen Path

Dzogchen is a profound tradition, but it is also an area where misconceptions and wrong views can easily arise.

Foundations of Dzogchen provides a carefully guided introduction to the essential principles and practices of Dzogchen, helping practitioners establish a clear foundation before progressing further along the path.

This is not a scholastic study of Dzogchen.

Instead, the course emphasizes meditative and experiential learning through direct teachings, contemplation and guided meditation.

The teachings and practices are offered by Lama Michael Gregory together with qualified Lamas who have agreed to assist practitioners along their respective journeys.

The Intention is Simple

To provide clear guidance and practical experience that can be brought directly into meditation and everyday life.

Four Ways to Study and Practise

Each part of the course is available to return to in a rhythm that supports your own practice.

13 In-Depth Video Teachings

The course includes 13 recorded lectures on the Foundations of Dzogchen, with each class incorporating teaching, contemplation and guided meditation.

Each class is approximately 1.5–2 hours and can be watched at your own pace.

For the best experience, it is recommended that you watch approximately one class each week and then spend the following week working with the practices.

Class Notes & Practice Materials

Detailed class notes accompany the teachings, helping you review the main points and deepen your understanding.

Notes, homework and meditation materials can also be downloaded from the individual classes.

Quizzes, Exercises & Reflection

Course activities provide opportunities to reflect on what you have learned and engage more directly with the course concepts and practices.

Meditation Guidance & Support

Vajragarbha members have access to personal meditation instructors who provide weekly mentoring and email support for meditation practice.
