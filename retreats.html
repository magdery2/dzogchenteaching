Questions

Before You Register

Do members get a discount?

Yes. Members receive discounted pricing on every retreat, in person or online, across the full library.

Can I attend online instead of in person?

Most retreats offer a Zoom option with recorded access, so you can join live or catch up on your own schedule.

Are scholarships available?

A limited number of full and partial scholarships exist for students committed to the path but facing financial constraints. Email us before you register.

Where is the in-person retreat center?

Pemako Retreat Center is located in Julian, California, and hosts our in-person programs and solo retreats.
