## Purpose and non-negotiable principles

The website exists to help people find a genuine path out of suffering and to carry the teaching lineage forward. Growth and revenue support that purpose; they do not outrank it.

- Copy follows Right Speech: true, kind, useful, timely, plain, warm, and unhurried.
- Never exploit suffering, fear, inadequacy, spiritual urgency, or FOMO.
- Do not make health, treatment, attainment, transmission, or realisation claims.
- Never invent testimonials, lineage claims, benefits, dates, prices, scholarships, or urgency.
- The free course is genuine dāna, not a hollow lead magnet. Paid pages should always leave a useful free path available.
- State prerequisites early and clearly. They protect students and lineage and are never conversion friction to hide.
- Present Lama Michael as an experienced practitioner and guide, not through guru inflation.
- Intensive-retreat material needs honest suitability and safety language.
- Accessibility and clarity are part of the site's service, not optional polish.

Before shipping, ask:

1. Is every claim true, including what it implies?
2. Does this serve the reader even if they never pay?
3. Would Lama Michael say this to a student's face?
4. Does the visitor leave calmer and with a true next step?

## Agreed strategic direction

- Replace the overwhelming catalogue of 30+ courses with a curated path taught in order.
- Make the Dzogchen pathway the structural spine, with Foundations of Dzogchen as an important entry point and a small set of flagship courses rather than a catalogue.
- Make the free entry point easy to find.
- Show Lama Michael, his background, teaching style, and verifiable lineage early enough to establish trust.
- Surface the living community: weekly meditations, Sacred Sundays, Tuesday Dzogchen teachings, spiritual-friendship/sangha classes, retreats, and the physical centre.
- Give visitors short, appropriate video encounters with Lama Michael. Christophe/Chris sets direction and selects moments; Ayo handles editing, with Claire able to advise on teaching appropriateness.
- Treat the website mainly as reach and capture. Email nurtures; live courses convert; membership deepens the relationship.
- Distinguish cold and warm audiences. Do not push membership as the first action for a new visitor.
- The primary owned channel is a calm weekly Dharma email: one teaching and one relevant happening.
- Use privacy-respecting analytics when introduced, and measure clarity and usefulness rather than chasing conversion at the expense of values.

## Facebook and Instagram community research

Detailed research reports are stored in the active workspace:

- `output/pdf/Vajragarbha-Facebook-Community-and-Membership-Strategy.pdf`
- `output/pdf/Vajragarbha-Instagram-Community-and-Membership-Strategy.pdf`

These reports contain dated public snapshots, not permanent account analytics. Recheck the platforms or obtain Meta Insights whenever current figures, audience demographics, reach, saves, shares, watch time, link clicks, or attribution matter.

Public snapshot recorded on 20 August 2026:

- Lama Michael's visible Facebook profile showed approximately 21,000 followers and 912 following.
- His verified Instagram account, `@mindmichael`, showed approximately 36,800 followers and 5,714 following.
- Facebook appeared stronger for conversation, announcements, community milestones, retreats, and relationship signals.
- Instagram appeared stronger for reach, discovery, frequent contemplative teaching, saves/lightweight engagement, and introducing Lama Michael to a wider international audience.
- Across both channels, people responded most clearly to complete practical teachings, authentic lineage material, direct practice language, and human glimpses of Lama Michael. Conventional promotional announcements generated less conversation.
- The clearest lifecycle is: social teaching and discovery -> relevant website page or free course -> email and live teaching -> membership for people ready for ongoing practice.
- Do not position membership as access to “more content,” because both platforms already provide abundant free teachings. Position it as a living rhythm of guided practice, teaching, spiritual friendship, continuity, and a clear path taught in order.
- This audience expects depth, lineage integrity, warm non-pressured communication, explicit prerequisites, international time-zone and recording clarity, continued free access, and honest information about what happens after joining.
- Facebook and Instagram comments are useful evidence of warmth, trust, questions, enrollment intent, and community resonance, but most are not verified student outcomes. Never publish a comment as a testimonial without confirming the person's relationship to the teaching and obtaining explicit permission for the wording and attribution.
- When creating social strategy, let Facebook teach and convene, let Instagram create recognition and discovery, let the website orient, let email nurture, let a free or live experience establish fit, and let membership deepen an existing relationship.
