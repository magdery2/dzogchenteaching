Your Teacher

About Lama Michael Gregory

Lama Michael Gregory is a Buddhist teacher whose training spans more than three decades across the Indo-Tibetan and Theravāda traditions. Beginning his studies in the early 1990s, he apprenticed under many of the greatest meditation masters of Tibet, including HH The Dalai Lama, and his root teacher from his teenage years — HH Penor Rinpoche.

He teaches the complete Buddhist path to liberation, following the traditional stages of Lam Rim, and personally guides students from their first class toward the practices suited to them.
