The Complete Teachings

One Path, From First Sit to the Great Perfection

A comprehensive, interactive course system on the complete Buddhist path to liberation, following the traditional Lam Rim stages, ancient wisdom applied with practical, modern clarity.

Vajragarbha offers more than twenty courses across the Theravāda, Mahayana, Vajrayana, and Dzogchen traditions, taught by Lama Michael and a small circle of seasoned teachers, including Lama Philip, Lama Kinley, and others with years of teaching experience and deep retreat knowledge. Every course is available live via Zoom and on-demand afterward, so you can study on your own schedule without losing the immediacy of live transmission.

Most students move through the stages below in sequence, live and in community, with Lama Michael guiding each person toward the right next step. If you're new here, the free Mindfulness course is where everyone begins.
