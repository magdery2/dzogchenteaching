The Transmission of the Great Perfection Tantras

Live Tuesdays with Lama Michael, this is the core weekly Dzogchen class and a direct introduction to the nature of mind. Alongside it, Vajragarbha hosts a rare, complete transmission of the Dzogchen Great Perfection tantras in English with Lama Chris Wilkinson, carrying empowerment cycles originally received from Dilgo Khyentse Rinpoche and Dagchen Rinpoche.

As the teaching itself puts it: “This is about empowering yourself. Let this be a reminder that these tantras are a reflection of your own nature. You don't need permission to be ready.”

Six Transmission Collections

What's Being Transmitted

Eight Early Tantras of the Great Perfection

Including feminine enlightenment presentations: the Vajra Yoginī Tantra, Aralī, and Mārīcī texts.

The Pearl Necklace Tantra

King Hayagrīva addresses Vajradhāra on the Great Perfection's application to contemporary life.

The Six Spaces of the All Good Upadesha Tantra

Telepathic and verbal communications between the All Good One and bodhisattvas across six realms.

The Eighteen Early Tantras

A comprehensive collection from the Fifth and Thirteenth Later Translation periods.

Perfect Wisdom: Four Early Tantras

Space Section texts from Vairochana's eighth-century translations.

The All-Creating King: A Root Tantra

Considered the most important Great Perfection source, in eighty-four chapters.

About Lama Chris Wilkinson

Lama Christopher Wilkinson began Dharma studies at fifteen under Dezhung Rinpoche, and went on to earn degrees in Asian Languages and Literature and Comparative Religion from the University of Washington, and a master's degree from the University of Calgary. He has taught as adjunct professor at the University of Calgary and served as a research fellow with the Centre de Recherches sur les Civilisations de l'Asie Orientale.

His primary teachers include Geshe Ngawang Nornang, Lama Kalu Rinpoche, Dilgo Khyentse Rinpoche, and Nyoshul Khen Rinpoche, and he identifies early with the Rimé non-sectarian movement. Since 2012, he has released more than thirty volumes of Great Perfection tantra translations, beginning with the seven-volume Sakya Kongma series.
