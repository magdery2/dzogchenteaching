“Guru Yoga becomes indistinguishable from the path itself.” Twelve-week courses taught live via Zoom, with full recordings and lifetime access.

Every course includes full platform access, daily morning meditation sessions, guided practices, a private online community, and lifetime access to the material once it's yours.
