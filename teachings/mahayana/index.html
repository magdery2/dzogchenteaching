The Path of the Bodhisattva

Mahayana Teachings

“The moment you have realized bodhicitta, you have become a Mahayana practitioner and are on the path to complete enlightenment.” — The Dalai Lama

The Mahayana path turns practice outward: not away from the mind, but toward every being who shares it. These courses build the classical foundation, bodhicitta and the six perfections, through close study of root texts alongside guided meditation and live discussion.

20-Week Course

Buddha Nature

Third Turning of the Buddha's Wheel of Dharma. An in-depth exploration of Buddha Nature as our innate potential for awakening, drawn from the Mahayana Uttaratantra Shastra and the Five Treatises of Maitreya, across Zen, Yogācāra, and Tibetan traditions.

  • Live Thursdays, 7–9pm EST via Zoom
  • Close textual reading, guided meditation, Q&A
  • Recordings posted to Teachable
  • Taught by Lama Pemba (Phillip)
