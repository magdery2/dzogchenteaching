Skillful Means

Tantra Teachings

“Whatever actions you engage in, do not do anything non-dharmic that fails to become the accumulation of merit and wisdom.” — Guru Padmasambhava

Vajragarbha offers transformative, classically precise instruction from the lower tantras through Vajrayoginī, Chakrasamvara, and other Highest Yoga Tantra teachings. Because of the nature of this material, access is limited to members who have received empowerment and authorization from Lama Michael and Lama Philip.

If you feel called to this practice, email us with your background and current practice, and we'll be in touch about next steps.
