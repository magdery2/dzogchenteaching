Retreats

Wise, transformative, and compassionate Buddhist retreats drawing on the Vajrayana, Dzogchen, and Theravāda traditions, in person at Pemako or live online.

Every program on the calendar is available by Zoom, with in-person options at the Pemako retreat center in San Diego for those who can join. Formats range from one-day sits to the annual thirty-day Dzogchen summer retreat, each one designed to support your practice at the stage you're actually in.
