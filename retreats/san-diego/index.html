Pemako Retreat Center

Nestled among the golden hills of San Diego's countryside, on a placid lake, our in-person home for retreat and renewal.

Pemako sits above Lake Cuyamaca in Julian, California, a sanctuary of rest and renewal for every in-person retreat on the calendar, from single-day sits to the annual thirty-day summer retreat. It's a place built for depth: quiet enough to hear the mind settle, spacious enough to hold weeks of continuous practice.

The grounds combine a dedicated meditation hall, simple comfortable accommodation, and the kind of natural quiet that's difficult to find, and easy to sink into, once you arrive.
