The Jewel Maker: The Reverberation of Sound

The 2026 annual thirty-day Dzogchen summer retreat, led directly by Lama Michael at Pemako or live via Zoom.

This summer's retreat is a thirty-day Dzogchen immersion centered on the Reverberation of Sound Tantra, from the Seventeen Tantras of the Dzogchen Menngagde. Within the Nyingthig lineage, this tantra is regarded as a pinnacle teaching: “the king of the tantras is the Consequence,” the text says of itself, the most secret and unsurpassed teaching, offered as direct instruction rather than symbolism.

Sound, here, is understood as an expression of awareness itself, not merely something heard. The teaching introduces nāda, primordial sound, and methods like semdzin, holding the mind, to stabilize recognition. Lama Michael offers the oral transmission (lung) of the tantra and provides practice instruction (tri), leading participants into direct recognition through immersion rather than concept.

This is not conceptual training but sustained immersion: nothing is cultivated, improved, or abandoned. Sound itself becomes the path, and the realization.

What the Thirty Days Looks Like

  • Four extended daily practice sessions, held in continuous silence except for Q&A and essential communication
  • No phone or internet use during the retreat
  • The Five Precepts observed throughout
  • Vegetarian meals for in-person participants, supported by karma yoga
  • Integration practice across waking, dreaming, and deep sleep states

Full participation from May 22 through June 20 is required. Exceptions for timezones or medical needs may be made on inquiry. This retreat is intended for students with an intermediate to advanced Dzogchen background; if you haven't attended a retreat with Lama Michael before, please contact us before registering.
