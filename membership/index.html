Vajragarbha: Dzogchen Sangha Membership

Once you've found your footing on the path, membership is the home for ongoing practice: full access to every live class, the recorded library, and direct connection to the community.

What's Included

Everything, for One Monthly Rate

  • Live Dzogchen, Tantra & Mahayana Classes: full access to current live course offerings, plus recordings and materials on Teachable.
  • Live Classes Every Day: Theravāda, Mahayana, Vajrayana and Tantra teachings with experienced teachers.
  • Discounted Retreats: access to hundreds of Zoom retreats, live and recorded, taught by Lama Michael, at discounted member rates.
  • Private Sangha Group: daily teachings from Lama Michael, exclusively for members, in a private community space.
  • Weekly Dharma Mailer: one inspiring teaching a week, delivered to your inbox, not a flood of updates.
  • Weekly Instructor Meetings: group meditation guidance and a space to discuss your practice directly.
  • Discounted Mentoring: one-on-one retreat guidance and non-dual mentoring and coaching, at member rates.
  • Three-Year Retreat Access: recordings and support for the long-term retreat program, for those on that path.
